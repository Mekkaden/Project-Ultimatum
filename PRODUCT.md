# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Dedicated natural bodybuilders seeking a no-nonsense, high-friction-reducing training tracker.

## Product Purpose
To track workouts, sets (RIR), sleep, and cardio with zero friction, adhering strictly to a core doctrine.

## Positioning
A hardcore, offline-first (PWA) tracking experience without generic fluff, focusing purely on absolute progress and adherence to the protocol.

## Operating Context
Used in the gym during intense training sessions where speed, reliability, and minimal distraction are critical. Must operate flawlessly offline.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Progressive Web App (PWA) built with Next.js and Tailwind CSS. Leverages `localStorage` for instantaneous offline-first data persistence, with optional background sync to Supabase. Strict dark mode constraint.

## Brand Commitments
Intense, unyielding, "doctrine-first" tone. Aesthetic must be minimalist, dense, "stiff", high-contrast, and cyberpunk-inspired without relying on generic AI-template fluff.

## Evidence on Hand
Core tracking systems (Arena, Sleep, Cardio, Workouts) are fully operational and utilize local storage logic.
